Spoken clips for words and sentences
====================================

These clips are synthetic speech, not recordings of a person.

  Engine  VOICEVOX (voicevox_core 0.17.1)   https://voicevox.hiroshiba.jp/
  Voice   四国めたん (ノーマル)
  Credit  VOICEVOX:四国めたん

Terms (from TERMS.txt shipped with the VOICEVOX voice models, version 0.17.1)
  - Speech made with the 四国めたん voice may be used commercially and
    non-commercially as long as the credit "VOICEVOX:四国めたん" is shown.
    The site shows it in the page footer.
  - The voice has its own detailed terms at https://zunko.jp/con_ongen_kiyaku.html
  - Not allowed: reverse engineering the models, acts that harm the makers or
    third parties, and use against public order and morals.
  - Anyone who reuses these clips has to follow the same terms.

How they are made
  tools/say-audio/build.py reads the lessons, synthesises each word and sentence,
  checks that the engine's reading matches the lesson's kana, trims and levels the
  result and saves it here. index.json maps each text to its file.

The site owner agreed to this voice and credit on 2026-11-23.
