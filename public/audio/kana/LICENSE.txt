Kana pronunciation clips (a.mp3 ... zu.mp3)
==========================================

What they are
  One clip per Japanese kana sound: 45 basic sounds, 23 voiced and half-voiced
  sounds, 33 combination sounds (kya, sha, ...), 101 files. Hiragana and katakana
  share a clip. を plays o.mp3, ぢ plays ji.mp3 and づ plays zu.mp3, because they
  are pronounced the same as お, じ and ず.
  Each clip has two people saying the sound once each: a woman, then a man.

Where they come from
  Recordings made for Tae Kim's Guide to Learning Japanese
  https://guidetojapanese.org/learn/grammar/hiragana
  The guide is released under a Creative Commons
  Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike licence (stated at
  https://guidetojapanese.org/learn/?p=37).

  The files were taken from the copies used by the DJT Kana quiz
  (https://djtguide.neocities.org/kana/), as kept unchanged in
  https://github.com/hechangjia/kana-study (assets/audio-djt).

  What was checked: the 46 basic sounds are the same recordings, under the same
  names, as the files credited to guidetojapanese.org in
  https://github.com/coregameHD/Gojuuon. The voiced and combination sounds have
  the same two voices, the same layout and the same encoding, and Tae Kim's page
  offers sound for those tables too, so they are taken to be from the same set;
  that part was not compared against the original site directly.

Changes made here
  Each original file is the woman's take, a long pause, then the man's take
  (1.2 to 4.1 seconds). Here the two takes were cut out, set to the same
  loudness, joined with a short pause, and saved as mono MP3. Nothing else was
  altered: no change of speed or pitch, no noise removal.
